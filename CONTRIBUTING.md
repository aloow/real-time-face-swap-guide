# Contributing

Sourced corrections, useful tools, reproducible compatibility reports, and translations are welcome. The same standards apply to the maintainer's product and other projects.

## Add or correct a tool

- Explain the exact task: photo, prerecorded video, live webcam, portrait animation, or character replacement.
- Link to a primary source and identify the version or review date.
- Distinguish documented features from features you personally reproduced.
- Describe installation, processing location when known, recording, and output routing separately.
- Disclose authorship, employment, sponsorship, or any other relationship with the tool.
- Keep descriptions factual. Avoid ranking claims, unverified performance figures, affiliate links, tracking links for third-party tools, and keyword repetition.
- Put archived projects in the legacy section. For a project with little recent activity, report the evidence without implying active maintenance.
- Do not submit copied marketing pages, private materials, model weights, executables, or personal face media.

There is no minimum Stars requirement here. Relevance, evidence, and usefulness determine inclusion. A submission does not guarantee acceptance or endorsement.

## Report compatibility

Use the [test protocol](testing.md) in a compatibility issue. Report failures with the same care as successes. Do not include account details, API/session credentials, device serial numbers, identifying recordings, or private reference photos.

## Language and licensing

Keep the English and Chinese README summaries consistent. Link technical detail rather than duplicating unsupported claims across translations. Original contributions are made under the repository's MIT license. Link to third-party resources instead of copying their content or changing their license.

## Promotion

Project-related links are welcome where they help readers make a decision. Spam, paid placement, incentivized Stars, and unrelated self-promotion are outside this guide's scope. The maintainer's product relationship stays visible.
